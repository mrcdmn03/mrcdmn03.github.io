The originals were supplied with Project Gutenberg headers and licence terms. They are **not committed to this repository**; obtain the full UTF-8 texts from the linked source pages, or use the copies retained in your original coursework ZIP. Save each using the exact filenames above in this folder.

These are the full ebooks **including Project Gutenberg headers and licence text**, not just the novel body. Source editions and their metadata influence word/sentence counts; later editions may differ from the coursework observations.

For verification of the exact archive copies received for this portfolio, see [`../results/source_checksums.json`](../results/source_checksums.json). A local copy of the complete texts is included in the separate, downloadable project ZIP provided to the author, with their Gutenberg licence text unchanged.
