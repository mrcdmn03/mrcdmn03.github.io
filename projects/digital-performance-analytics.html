LIVE BUSINESS EXPERIENCE · CASE STUDY 04

Digital performance analytics

Use Instagram Insights, YouTube Studio and Metricool to review reach, engagement and audience demographics and inform publishing decisions.

Context

Understand audience behaviour and content performance across multiple channels to guide content planning.

What I worked on

  • Monitored demographics, reach, engagement and content performance across Instagram, YouTube and TikTok.
  • Used Instagram Insights, YouTube Studio and Metricool in the reporting workflow.
  • Applied findings to publishing decisions, content planning and search optimisation.
  • Coordinated digital operations, collaborations and ongoing brand development.

Outcome

The Duman Family UK reached an Instagram audience of 30,000+ followers and 2.5 million views during a 30-day period. These are reported brand metrics, not an isolated estimate of the impact of any single change.
